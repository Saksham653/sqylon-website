Saksham Srivastava

Co-founder, Sqylon · Full Backend & Automation Developer

Saksham Srivastava is a full backend and automation developer and the co-founder of Sqylon, together with Shivam Jha. Saksham built the backend and automation of OSHT – Agro Trading, the grain-trade app and office system of Om Shri Hari Trading Pvt Ltd.

On OSHT – Agro Trading

  • Servers and APIs that the Android, iOS and web apps all talk to.
  • The database and the books: purchase, sale, ledgers, vouchers and payments kept as double-entry accounts that always balance.
  • Background workers that keep data in step across systems, every few minutes, on their own.
  • Automation: reading documents with OCR, matching payments to bills, and producing bills, statements and reports as PDFs.
  • Releases and reliability: zero-downtime deploys and health checks for a system the office uses all day.
