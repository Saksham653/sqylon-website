OSHT – Agro Trading

The order book for Om Shri Hari Trading's grain business, used in the field by brokers, suppliers and staff. Designed and developed by Saksham Srivastava and Shivam Jha of Sqylon.

GET IT ONGoogle Play DOWNLOAD ON THEApp Store

What it does

  • Raise an order one question at a time and watch it move through every stage, from approval to delivery.
  • Follow each lorry paper by paper, from loading to the factory's receipt and on to the pay slip.
  • See every paper: which of a lorry's papers are in, which one is still owed, and send yours by camera, gallery or PDF.
  • Know where your money is: purchase receipts and pay slips appear the moment the office files them.
  • Get told the moment something needs you, instead of having to check.

Built for the mandi

  • Hindi and English, with more Indian languages on the way.
  • Speak the quantity, the rate and any note, so an order can be raised without typing.
  • Reads questions aloud for anyone who would rather listen than read.
  • Works offline: holds on to papers when the signal drops and sends them when the bars come back.

About OSHT and who built it

Who developed the OSHT – Agro Trading app?

OSHT – Agro Trading was designed and developed by Saksham Srivastava (full backend and automation developer) and Shivam Jha (frontend and automation developer), the two founders of the software studio Sqylon, for Om Shri Hari Trading Private Limited.

Who built the backend of OSHT?

Saksham Srivastava built the backend: the servers and APIs, the database and accounting books, the background workers and the automation.

Who built the OSHT app's screens and frontend?

Shivam Jha built the frontend: the Android and iOS app and the web office console, and its automation for notifications and field use.

What is OSHT – Agro Trading?

It is the order book of Om Shri Hari Trading Pvt Ltd's grain business. Brokers and suppliers raise orders, follow each lorry from loading to the factory's receipt, send the papers behind it, and see purchase receipts and pay slips, in Hindi and English, with voice input.

Where can I download it?

Free on Google Play and the Apple App Store. An account with Om Shri Hari Trading is needed to sign in.
