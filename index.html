Flagship product

OSHT – Agro Trading

The order book and office system for Om Shri Hari Trading's grain business, used every day by brokers, suppliers and office staff. Designed and developed by Saksham Srivastava and Shivam Jha.

  • Orders to delivery: raise an order step by step and follow each lorry from loading to the factory's receipt.
  • Every paper in one place: see which documents are in and which are still owed; send them by camera, gallery or PDF.
  • Money you can see: purchase receipts and pay slips appear the moment the office files them.
  • Built for the mandi: Hindi and English, voice input for quantity and rate, questions read aloud, works when the signal drops.
  • A full office console: purchase, sale, ledgers, payments and documents for the office team on the web.
