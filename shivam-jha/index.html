Shivam Jha

Co-founder, Sqylon · Frontend & Automation Developer

Shivam Jha is a frontend and automation developer and the co-founder of Sqylon, together with Saksham Srivastava. Shivam built the frontend and automation of OSHT – Agro Trading, the grain-trade app and office system of Om Shri Hari Trading Pvt Ltd.

On OSHT – Agro Trading

  • The Android and iOS app that brokers and suppliers use in the field.
  • The web office console the office team works in all day.
  • Hindi-first screens with English alongside, built for the mandi and for cheap phones.
  • Voice and read-aloud: speak the quantity and rate, and hear questions read out.
  • Automation: notifications the moment something needs a person, and papers that send themselves once the signal is back.
